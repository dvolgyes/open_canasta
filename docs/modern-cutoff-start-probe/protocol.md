This exploratory probe checks the current provisional cutoff score at the start of previously recorded single-hand scenarios.
It uses the 24 hands from the depth pilot and 48 hands from the earlier eight-versus-two hand pilot and replication.
These are 36 paired deals with both team assignments, not 72 independent deals.
The datasets and this check were selected after other analyses, so they are not a final confirmation sample.

For each hand, reconstruct the original initial deal and symmetric starting score.
Compare the cutoff evaluator's team ordering at that initial state with the team that scored higher at the end of that same recorded hand.
The working prediction is at least 45 disagreements among the 72 hands.
Fewer disagreements do not establish that the evaluator is well calibrated or that it chooses good actions.
Report tied cutoff values and tied final hand scores separately.

The reconstruction uses the simulator's actual hidden cards, not the current player's information or a belief sample.
The final result is one continuation under the recorded policies, not a counterfactual result for every available action.
The starting-score bands and paired seats are fixed by the earlier studies.
This probe does not measure full-match win probability, establish that changing the cutoff improves play, or authorize LLM testing.
